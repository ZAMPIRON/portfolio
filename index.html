Featured project

FrequencyON

Attendance that takes itself. A facial-recognition totem that helps schools spot students at risk of dropping out before it's too late.

The problem

Manual roll calls waste time in every class, and paper lists rarely turn into information. By the time a school notices a student has stopped coming, they have often already dropped out.

What I built

Students walk past a tablet at the school entrance and their attendance is recorded automatically: no badges, no lists, no lines. A liveness check rejects printed photos. Every record feeds dashboards for teachers and coordinators that flag students below 75% attendance and send them email alerts, so the school can act early.

Technologies

  • Python
  • Flask
  • OpenCV
  • face_recognition (dlib)
  • SQLite / SQLAlchemy
  • JavaScript
  • Chart.js

My role

Lead Developer: I built the system end to end, from the facial-recognition service to the totem app and the teacher, student and admin dashboards. Faces are stored as numeric vectors and processed on the school's own server, with no images sent to third parties.
