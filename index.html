Featured project

FrequencyON

A facial-recognition attendance totem that helps my school fight student dropout.

The problem

School dropout often starts with students quietly missing classes. Manual roll calls are slow, and by the time anyone notices a pattern, it can be too late.

What I built

A totem at the school entrance that recognizes students' faces, records attendance automatically, and shows the school staff who is starting to miss classes so they can act early.

Technologies

  • Python
  • OpenCV
  • Facial recognition
  • SQL

My role

Lead Developer: I designed the system architecture, built the recognition pipeline, and led the development team.
